Compare the three approaches

Full composition: YuE2 plans both melody and chords before rendering. Choose this for a structured song or to guide it with melody and chord notation.

Melody first: YuE2 plans the melody, while the audio stage has more freedom over accompaniment. Try this when the tune matters most.

Direct audio: YuE2 generates audio directly from your sound description and lyrics, without the intermediate score plan. It is unavailable when you supply sheet music.

These are composition approaches, not quality levels. The same lyrics and style can produce different arrangements.

01 · MUSIC ON YOUR COMPUTER

Start with your local GPU.

Install the local YuE2 runtime and models with Setup YuE2.cmd, then check Local engines in Engine settings. Choose Local when creating a song. Local generation uses your hardware without cloud generation charges.

Use a style pill as a starting point, then describe the instruments, mood and arrangement. Add lyrics for vocals; empty lyrics request an instrumental, but the model may still produce vocals. Full composition plans melody and chords first; Direct audio skips the written plan.

Use Clear for new song to remove content from your previous draft. Choose a short target length for an intro, or Automatic for a natural ending within the selected maximum.

02 · VIDEOS WITH FAL.AI

Choose a model for your story.

Save a regular fal.ai API key in Engine settings. Select fal.ai when creating a video, choose your song and soundtrack version, then pick a video model. An optional separate admin key enables billing information.

Upload and label reference photos, such as Rusty or Beauty, and select them for models that support image references. Capabilities vary by model. Describe scenes using those labels.

Try a short preview before a full song to check the look. fal.ai generations are paid; review the model and estimated cost before generating. Completed videos stay in your Video library.

03 · KEEP YOUR IDEAS TOGETHER

Use projects and related assets.

Group songs and videos into projects. Your libraries keep lyric drafts, songs, videos and sheet music available for later. Lyrics show whether they have been used in a song.

Click Related assets to see how a draft, song, score and video connect. Check these connections before deleting an asset; a song used by another asset cannot be deleted.

04 · RUNPOD · ADVANCED · EXPERIMENTAL

Cloud compute takes extra care.

RunPod is an optional advanced workflow. End-to-end generation has not yet been verified, and GPU availability can prevent jobs from starting. Familiarize yourself with RunPod before using this section.

Saving an API key alone does not rent compute. Applying a reviewed setup can create billed storage; worker initialization and generation are also billed. Serverless with zero idle workers can scale down, while a running Pod continues charging even when idle. Network volumes and stopped Pod storage may still cost money.

Worker limits share your account allowance with other projects. One worker is usually enough for a single song; more may help parallel video clips. Closing the Studio does not stop cloud resources. Check your account after use.

2 · MODELS & COMPUTE

One shared network volume stores both models. Requires at least 200 GB; 300 GB recommended. Setup starts the YuE2 song engine and downloads MiniMax without starting a video service. Configure on-demand video workers afterward.

Automatic scans compatible GPUs with capacity in your volume’s region. The review shows the selected rates; this is a selection limit, not a permanent billing cap. MiniMax requires four H200 GPUs per worker.

Minimum: 0. Workers start for requests and scale down after 10 idle seconds. Songs use one GPU per worker; MiniMax uses four H200 GPUs. 10 is a cap, not a reservation or a promise of capacity.

RunPod’s worker allowance is shared across your account. Letting this endpoint use all 10 workers can leave other projects without capacity. For a single song, choose 1 worker; use more for parallel video clips. Automatic GPU scanning selects GPU types, not the worker count.

Creating storage and downloading a model are separate from starting video generation. Existing models and other volume files stay in place.
